## The Fundamental Buy Rules

| Term | Meaning | Goal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Full Buy** | Vandal/Phantom + Full Shields + All Utilities (~$3,900+). | Win the round with superior firepower. |
| **Half Buy** | Spending enough to stay competitive while ensuring you have ~$3,900 for next round. | Chip away at enemy shields/economy. |
| **Eco (Save)** | Spending little to nothing (usually <$1,500). | Guarantee a Full Buy in the following round. |
| **Bonus** | Keeping the weapons you survived with from the previous round instead of upgrading. | Damage the enemy's economy while saving your own credits. |

## Round-by-Round Strategy

### Phase 1: The Opening (Rounds 1–3)

#### **Round 1: Pistol Round (Starting Credits: $800)**
*   **Strategy:** You must spend your credits. Winning the pistol round usually secures the next two rounds.
*   **Duelists:** Ghost + Utility **OR** Frenzy + Small Shields.
*   **Initiators/Sentinels:** Ghost + Utility **OR** Classic + Full Shields + Utility.
*   **Controllers:** Ghost + Smokes **OR** Classic + Small Shields + All Smokes.

#### **Round 2: The Aftermath**
*   **CASE: If You WON Round 1:** 
    *   **The "Anti-Eco" Buy:** Buy **Spectre, Ares, or Marshal + Full Shields**. 
    *   *Why?* The enemy will likely have Classics; you want a fire-rate or range advantage to prevent an upset.
*   **CASE: If You LOST Round 1:** 
    *   **The "Eco" Save:** Buy nothing or a cheap Sheriff. **Do not buy shields.** Save your credits to ensure a Full Buy in Round 3.

#### **Round 3: The Pivot**
*   **CASE: If You WON Round 2:** 
    *   **The "Bonus":** Do **not** upgrade. Keep your Spectres/Marshals from Round 2. You are expected to lose this round to the enemy's Full Buy, but if you kill 2 or 3 of them, you destroy their economy.
*   **CASE: If You LOST Round 2:** 
    *   **Full Buy:** Buy **Vandal/Phantom + Full Shields**. This is your chance to take the lead back.

---

### Phase 2: Mid-Half (Rounds 4–12)

During these rounds, the buy depends entirely on your team's average credits. Look at the "Minimum Credits Next Round" indicator in the shop.

#### **If You Have Plenty of Credits ($4,500+):**
*   **Full Buy:** Vandal/Phantom, Full Shields, and all Utility.

#### **If You Are Broke ($1,500 - $2,500):**
*   **The "Save" Round:** Buy nothing or a Classic/P250. Aim for a "Hero Rifle" if one person is rich, but generally, everyone saves together.

#### **The "Force Buy" (Emergency):**
*   **Strategy:** If it is the last round of the half (Round 12) or you are down 0–7, spend **everything**. Buy SMGs, Shotguns, and Light Shields. Leaving money in the bank during the last round of a half is a waste.

---

### Phase 3: The Second Half (Rounds 13–24)

The logic resets at Round 13 (the second Pistol Round). Repeat the logic from Phase 1 and 2. 

*   **Round 13:** Pistol Buy (Same as Round 1).
*   **Round 14:** Anti-Eco (if won) or Save (if lost).
*   **Round 24 (Match Point):** Spend every single credit. There is no tomorrow.

## Agent-Specific Economy Nuances

While the roles follow the rules above, certain agents have unique interactions:

### **1. The "Cheap" Ult Agents (Jett, Chamber, Neon)**
*   **Strategy:** If your Ultimate is ready (Blade Storm, Tour De Force, Overdrive), you can **Save** while your team **Full Buys**. 
*   **Benefit:** You use your Ult as a "free" rifle, allowing you to drop a Vandal for a teammate who is poor.

### **2. The "Utility Heavy" Agents (Sage, Breach, Cypher)**
*   **Strategy:** Your utility is often more important than "Full Shields."

*   **Strategy:** The Operator costs **$4,700**. To afford this by Round 3 or 4, you must often buy very light (Classic/Small Shields) even if you win the first two rounds.

## The "Loss Streak" Rule (Rounds 4-12)
If your team is losing multiple rounds in a row, the game gives you more credits ($1,900 -> $2,400 -> $2,900).

1.  **Check "Min Next Round":** In the shop, look at the top left. If you have **$3,900** for next round, you can spend the rest now.
2.  **The Force Buy:** If the score is 2-7 or worse, stop saving. Buy **Judge/Stinger + Small Shields** every round to try and break the momentum.
3.  **Last Round of Half (Round 12/24):** Spend **$0**. Every credit disappears when sides switch. Buy an Operator or Odin even if you can't afford shields.
